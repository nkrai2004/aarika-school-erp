# AARIKA School ERP

**AARIKA — Adaptive Assessment & Revision Intelligence for Knowledge Advancement**

AARIKA is a school management and learning platform being built as a real, production-ready, multi-tenant school ERP/SIS platform.

## Product Direction

AARIKA is designed to bring school operations, academics, administration, attendance, duties, transport, maintenance, reporting, audit, and future AI capabilities into one governed platform.

## Build Principles

- Production-first architecture
- Multi-tenant by design
- Role-based access control
- Strong auditability
- Mobile-responsive experience
- English/Hindi readiness
- Secure authentication and data access
- Modular architecture without unnecessary duplication
- Automation and AI added only after the operational foundation is reliable
